## How I Approach Documentation

Good technical documentation should answer three basic questions:

1. What does this product or feature do?
2. How do I use it?
3. What should I do when something goes wrong?

I focus on clear structure, practical examples, consistent terminology, and documentation that helps the reader move from understanding to action.
